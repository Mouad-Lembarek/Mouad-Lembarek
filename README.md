## About me

I'm an **AI & Automation Engineer** based between **Marrakech** and **Casablanca**, Morocco. I mentor **Cylindrique**, a small team shipping AI tooling and SaaS products, and I'm an official **n8n Ambassador** — running meetups and hackathons across Morocco to grow the local automation & AI community.

- 🧠 Currently building **[Cylinbot](#)** — a multi-tenant AI support platform (Next.js, Supabase, Claude, RAG)
- 🤖 Also shipping a **CV Matching Platform** for recruiters, with explainable AI scoring
